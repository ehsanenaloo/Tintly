# Privacy

Tintly works only on your device. It collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no analytics, no account and no server.

Your settings stay in your browser. If you turn on sync, your browser copies them with your own account.

The enaloo.com pages that Tintly opens (welcome, survey after removal, feedback) get only the Tintly version, the language, the browser, the system and how long Tintly was installed in their link.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Storage | To keep your settings |
| Scripting | To color the pages you open |
| Access to all sites | To color the pages you open |
| Context menu, optional | For the menu item, if you turn it on |
| Alarms, optional | For a schedule, if you allow it |

## Delete your data

Use **Reset all settings** in **Settings**, or remove the extension.
