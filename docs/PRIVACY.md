# Privacy and data

Tintly collects nothing and sends nothing about you. It has no analytics, no account and no remote settings.

## What it reads

Tintly reads the style sheets, the colors and the pictures of the pages you open, so it can change their colors. It does not read what you type, your forms, your cookies or your history.

## What it keeps

Your settings, site rules, presets and site fixes are kept in your browser. If you turn on sync, your browser copies them through your own account. The language of the pages stays on the computer.

## What it sends

The only requests Tintly makes are to download a style sheet or a picture of the page you are on, when the browser does not let the page code read it. It asks for no address that the page did not already use, and it sends no cookies with the request.

## Pages on enaloo.com

After a fresh install, Tintly opens a welcome page on enaloo.com once, in a tab behind the one you are on. When you remove Tintly, the browser opens a short survey page. The links **Report a problem** and **Suggest a feature** in **About** open a feedback page. These addresses carry only the Tintly version, the language, the browser and its version, the system (such as win or mac) and how long ago Tintly was installed. Tintly sends nothing to these pages itself.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Storage | To keep your settings |
| Scripting | To add the color engine to the pages you open, and to put it into the tabs that are already open when you install or update Tintly or turn a site on |
| Access to all sites | To color the pages you open |
| Context menu, optional | To show the menu item, only if you turn it on |
| Alarms, optional | To switch pages on and off at the times of your schedule, without loading anything on pages that are off. Only if you allow it |

In Firefox the context menu permission is called `menus`. Firefox cannot ask for it or for the alarms permission later, so Tintly has both from the start. Neither adds a warning at install. Tintly shows the menu item only if you turn it on, and it sets alarms only when you use a schedule by hours or by sunset and sunrise.

## Delete your data

Use **Reset all settings** in **Settings**, **Backup and data**, or remove the extension. Both remove what Tintly keeps in your browser.
