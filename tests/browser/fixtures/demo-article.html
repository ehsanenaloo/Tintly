Why the evening is the best time to read

By Mara Ellis, 6 minute read

When the light goes down, a white page becomes a lamp pointed at your face. Many readers move to a dark theme after sunset, and the change is easy to feel in the first minute.

Good reading light is soft, steady and warm. A screen can be all three if you set it up once.

Start with the brightness. Then pick a text color that is easy on the eyes, not pure white. A short test is to read one paragraph aloud. If your eyes sting, lower the contrast a little.
